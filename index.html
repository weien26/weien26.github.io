Which Demonstration, When to Switch, and What to Do: Chaining a Frozen In-Context Policy

Nga Wei En, supervised by Yoonchang Sung
NTU Final Year Project, 2026

video: agent retries a failed step

Instant Policy can perform a skill from two demonstrations, but it can't pick its own demonstrations or tell when a skill is done. I kept it frozen and added a Selector (which demonstrations), a Monitor (when to switch) and a vision-language agent that retries failures, then ran a four-stage open–place–close chain in RLBench. A training-free stack beat a learned one on unseen object sizes (85% vs 74% chain success).

π0.5 on a Real Franka FR3

Black Sesame Technologies, 2026

Generated 641 pick demonstrations in Isaac Lab and fine-tuned π0.5 on them, reaching 96% success over a 15 × 15 cm workspace in simulation (hover over or tap the clip to see it). On the real arm, I ran the policy through a ROS 2 bridge at 10 Hz, fine-tuned on demonstrations from a teach-and-repeat recorder, and built an agentic planner around the frozen policy (SAM 3 segmentation and analytic kinematics) for multi-step tasks such as cube stacking.

Dexterous Hand Teleoperation

Black Sesame Technologies, 2026

Worked out the CAN protocol of an Inspire RH56 hand by testing (the shipped manuals were for other models), wrote a driver with a grip-force limit, and drove it from a single camera with MediaPipe. Moving arm teleoperation onto an impedance controller cut command-to-motion latency from 800 ms to 38 ms.

STM32 Sensor-Driven Mobile Robot

NTU Multidisciplinary Design Project (team), 2026

Wrote the STM32 motion firmware for a car-like robot that drives to and photographs obstacle markers: encoder-synchronised wheel speeds, gyro heading correction for straight runs and set-angle turns, IR and ultrasonic range checks, and a framed UART command protocol to the Raspberry Pi.
